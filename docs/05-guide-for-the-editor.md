# How to update the website

This is the only page you need. Nothing here can break anything.

## Getting in

Go to **gigicollective.com/admin** and sign in with GitHub. Bookmark that address.

You will stay signed in, so most of the time it opens straight to the editor.

## What the sections are

Down the left hand side there are four things:

**Site settings**: things that show up on every page. The site name, the footer, your
contact email, and everything on the front page. Two entries: *General* and *Front page*.

**Pages**: standalone pages like About or Contact. These are the ones that appear in the
menu at the top of the site.

**Blog posts**: dated articles. Newest appears first.

**Work**: projects or photo galleries.

## Writing something new

1. Click the section you want, for example **Blog posts**.
2. Click the **+** or **Create new** button.
3. Fill in the title, then write in the big box at the bottom.
4. Press **Publish**.

That is it. The website updates itself about a minute later. Refresh the page to see it.

## The fields explained

**Title**: the heading of the piece, and what makes its web address. Try to get this right
first time, because changing it later changes the address, and any link anyone saved will
stop working.

**Short summary**: one or two sentences. This is what shows up underneath the title in the
list of posts, and it is what Google puts under the link in search results. Worth writing
properly.

**Header image**: optional. Sits across the top of the page.

**Draft**: turn this **on** to hide something from the live site while you work on it. Turn
it **off** to make it visible. A draft is saved and safe, it just does not appear publicly.

**Tags**: optional, groups related posts together.

**Menu position** (Pages only): a number. Lower numbers sit further to the left in the top
menu. So `1` is first, `2` is second.

## Adding pictures

Inside the writing box, drag a photo straight in, or click the image button.

Two things that make a real difference:

- **Shrink big photos first.** A photo straight off a phone can be 5 megabytes, which makes
  the page slow to load. Around 1500 pixels wide is plenty. Preview on a Mac can do this:
  open the photo, Tools, Adjust Size.
- **Give it a sensible name before uploading.** `lavender-workshop.jpg` is better than
  `IMG_4471.jpg`, both for finding it later and for Google.

## Making a change to something that already exists

Click it in the list, change what you want, press Publish. Same as creating.

## Deleting

Open the item, then look for **Delete** in the menu. It disappears from the site on the next
rebuild.

Deleted things are recoverable, so it is not a disaster if you delete the wrong thing. Just
say so and it can be brought back.

## Things that are worth knowing

**Changes take about a minute.** If you refresh straight away and nothing has changed, wait
thirty seconds and try again. It is not broken.

**A hard refresh helps.** If it still looks old after a couple of minutes, hold Shift and
click reload.

**You cannot break the website from here.** The worst that happens is a page looks wrong, and
that is fixable in a minute.

**Editing on a phone works.** The editor is designed for it. Handy for fixing a typo.

## When to ask for help

- You want something the editor does not have a field for (a new type of page, a different
  layout, a change to how something looks)
- Something looks wrong on the live site and republishing does not fix it
- The sign-in stops working

None of these are things to try to fix yourself. Just ask.
